2. What data we process

When you register and use the service, we collect/process the following data:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • For a partner (reseller) account: company name, tax number, billing and shipping address
  • The content of the wedding page you create (the couple's names, wedding date, schedule, messages, uploaded cover photo)
  • Payment-related data (payments are processed by Stripe; we do not store card details ourselves)
  • Technical data (IP address, browser identifier), only to the extent allowed by your cookie settings
  • Advertising source data (UTM parameters, ad-click identifier, the page you came from), only with marketing consent and linked to your registration

3. Purpose and legal basis of processing

  • Account creation and providing the service (creating/managing the wedding page) — legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Contacting you by phone or email (e.g. information, offers, customer support) — legal basis: the consent you give at registration.
  • Billing and payment processing — legal basis: performance of a contract and legal (accounting) obligations.
  • Advertising measurement and optimization (Meta/Facebook Pixel and Conversions API, only if you give marketing consent in the cookie settings) — legal basis: consent. In this case, upon registration and purchase we send Meta your email address, phone number, name and account ID (irreversibly hashed), as well as your IP address, browser identifier and ad-click identifiers (these unhashed, as Meta requires). Without consent, this data is neither stored nor transmitted. If you withdraw consent later, let us know at the contact address and we will delete the advertising data linked to your account and send no further events.

4. Who we share data with

We share data, to the extent necessary, with the following processors acting on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, database and delivery of the website
  • Resend — sending transactional emails (e.g. confirmations)
  • Stripe — processing card payments
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook/Instagram) — only with marketing consent, for advertising measurement and optimization (hashed personal data plus IP address and browser identifier)

We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties for any other purpose.

5. How long we keep data

We retain your data for as long as your account exists, and after deletion for as long as required by legal obligations (e.g. 8 years for accounting records).

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, port your data, and object, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise your rights or file a complaint, contact us at the address above, or contact your local data protection supervisory authority.
